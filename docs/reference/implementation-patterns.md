## Benchmark Analysis of Major Custodians

| Provider | Regulatory Charter | Key Architecture | Primary Target Market | Core Differentiator |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Coinbase Custody** | NYDFS Trust Company (Qualified Custodian) | Offline Cold Storage & MPC Warm Tiers | Institutional Asset Managers, ETFs | Integrated Staking & Governance |
| **Anchorage Digital** | OCC Federal Bank Charter | MPC + Hardware Isolation | Banks, Sovereign Funds, Fintechs | Full Federal Banking Services & Settlement |

| **BNY Mellon / State Street** | Global Systemically Important Banks (G-SIBs) | Hybrid Legacy Custody + Digital Rails | Institutional Pension Funds & Asset Owners | Unified View of Traditional & Digital Assets |
