# Market Dynamics: Challenges and Strategic Opportunities

## Key Challenges & Operational Caveats

1. **Regulatory Fragmentation:**
   - Navigating shifting landscape between US Federal Reserve/OCC guidelines, legislative acts (CLARITY Act, GENIUS Act), and EU MiCA enforcement.
2. **Insurance Capacity Limits:**
   - Insurance policies for large Assets Under Custody (AUC) remain capped well below total balances, shifting residual risk to custody balance sheets.
3. **MPC Vendor Lock-in & Switching Costs:**
   - Architectural dependence on proprietary MPC key-share schemes creates high vendor lock-in and migration friction.
4. **Legacy Interoperability Deficit:**
   - Bridging blockchain-native accounting ledgers with traditional bank core accounting platforms (SWIFT, ISO 20022) remains partially fragmented.

## Revenue & Strategic Opportunities

1. **Tokenization & Collateral Management:**
   - Capitalising on automated issuance and lifecycle management of tokenized treasury bills, money market funds, and private credit.
2. **Margin Bundling (Custody + Settlement + Staking):**
   - Pure custody fees compress over time; yield generation via staking and instant off-exchange settlement captures expanding margins.
3. **Banks as Enterprise Clients:**
   - White-label DCA-as-a-Service allows traditional tier-2 and regional banks to offer digital custody without multi-year infrastructure builds.
